I'm Yuri! I'm from Chiba, Japan. I went to primary school in Malaysia and I've lived in Melbourne for about 9 years.

I've been working as a Japanese teacher for almost 8 years.

I have so much passion for teaching Japanese and making fun. Japan is such a great country of “Omotenashi”, the Manners and the culture I would love to teach it.

I am licensed for Primary and Special schools. I'm able to teach adults as well as children (iB/VCE/any school materials).

I can't wait to see you in SaCI.
