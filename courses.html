Courses

We offer Japanese language lessons for all levels, from beginner through to advanced learners. All courses are available face-to-face in Melbourne, or online anywhere in the world.

From an initial consultation and level assessment, we help you choose the lesson type that best suits your goals and experience.

All our classes are designed to help you improve your speaking, listening, reading, and writing skills in a fun and engaging way.

  • Private General Japanese Lessons
  • Group General Japanese Lessons
  • JLPT Preparation
  • VCE/IB Tutoring

Private Japanese lessons

Private lessons are fully flexible and tailored to your needs. We work with you to create a learning plan that matches your goals, whether that is general Japanese, business Japanese, or improving a specific skill like speaking or reading.

Beginners typically start with our recommended textbook みんなの日本語ⅠJapanese for Everyone: Book 1（赤本）Red Book though other materials can be used depending on your preferences.

Key features:

  • Learn at your own pace
  • Face-to-face or online
  • Flexible scheduling
  • Choice of textbooks

Group Japanese lessons

Group lessons are small, friendly classes of 2-5 students. Lessons are interactive and collaborative, allowing students to learn together and support each other's progreess.

Groups generally study the みんなの日本語Japanese for Everyone series of textbooks.

Key features:

  • Small class sizes
  • Learn with others at your level
  • Face-to-face or online
  • New students welcome

If there is no suitable group at your level, we may recommend private lessons or place you on a waiting list.

JLPT Preparation

These private lessons are designed for students preparing for the Japanese Language Proficiency Test (JLPT). Lessons focus on identifying strengths and weaknesses and improving performance in specific areas to raise your overall score.

Key features:

  • Targeted exam preparation
  • Can use your own reference books
  • Flexible scheduling
  • Face-to-face or online

VCE/IB Tutoring

We support VCE and IB students by teaching directly from their school textbooks and materials. Lessons focus on improving exam performance through targeted practice and study strategies tailored to student's needs and school requirements

Key features:

  • Tailored to your school's curriculum
  • Individual learning pace
  • Focus on improving exam performance
  • Face-to-face or online
