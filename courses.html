Courses

We offer Japanese language lessons for all levels, from beginner through to advanced learners. All courses are available face-to-face in Melbourne, or online anywhere in the world.

From an initial consultation and level assessment, we help you choose the lesson type that best suits your goals and experience.

All our classes are designed to help you improve your speaking, listening, reading, and writing skills in a fun and engaging way.

  • Private General Japanese Lessons
  • Group General Japanese Lessons
  • JLPT Tutoring
  • VCE Tutoring
