About

A small school with a big welcome.

幸sa-chi

We’re the Sakura Communication Institute — but you can call us SaCI for short! In Japanese this means happiness and luck — it’s pronounced sa-chi and written as 幸.

Based in Melbourne, we provide Japanese language courses to students all over the world. From total beginners to JLPT candidates and VCE/IB students — we’ll meet you at your level.

Unsure which lesson is right for you? Or already know some Japanese but unsure of your level?

Start with an initial consultation and level assessment and we’ll find the lesson style that suits you best. Reach out →

Lesson types

Four ways to study with us.

01 / Privateプライベート

Private lessons

Fully flexible — we build a learning plan that suits you. Business Japanese, travel Japanese, focus on listening & speaking — you choose. Beginners’ textbook: みんなの日本語Ⅰ（赤本）.

  • ✓Flexible schedule
  • ✓Face-to-face & online
  • ✓Choose from several textbooks
  • ✓Advance at your own pace
02 / Groupグループ

Group lessons

Small groups of 3–6 students. Big fun, interactive, friendly & cosy — everyone works together to reach the goal. Curriculum: みんなの日本語Ⅰ ➜ Ⅱ（赤本・青本）.

  • ✓New students always welcome
  • ✓Face-to-face & online
  • ✓Small, friendly groups (3–6)
  • ✓If no level-match, you’ll be offered private
03 / JLPT日本語能力試験

JLPT tutoring

Private tutoring focused on the Japanese Language Proficiency Test. We map your strengths and weak points, then concentrate on the weak areas to raise your overall score.

  • ✓Flexible schedule
  • ✓Face-to-face & online
  • ✓Use your own reference books
  • ✓Focus on the areas you want to improve
04 / VCE & IB学校サポート

VCE / IB tutoring

We teach from the textbook students are studying at school and aim to raise scores in school exams — working out trends, countermeasures, and study suited to each school.

  • ✓Flexible schedule
  • ✓Face-to-face & online
  • ✓Use your school materials
  • ✓Advance at your own pace
