Welcome to SaCI (Sakura Communication Institute), a Japanese language school based in Melbourne, Australia. We offer Japanese lessons for learners of all levels, both in person and online, and work with students from Australia and around the world.

Our lessons focus on building practical language skills—speaking, listening, reading, and writing—at a pace that suits each learner.
