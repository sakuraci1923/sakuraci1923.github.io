Welcome to SaCI (Sakura Communication Institute), a Japanese language school based in Melbourne, Australia. We offer Japanese lessons for learners of all levels, both in person and online, and work with students from Australia and around the world.

Our lessons focus on building practical language skills—speaking, listening, reading, and writing—at a pace that suits each learner.

Courses

We offer a variety of Japanese language courses for all levels, from beginner to advanced. Our classes are designed to help you improve your speaking, listening, reading, and writing skills in a fun and engaging way.

Face-to-face and online available teaching styles: Private General Japanese Lessons Group General Japanese Lessons JLPT Tutoring VCE Tutoring If you’re unsure which type of lesson to choose, or you already know some Japanese but are unsure of your level — from an initial consultation and level assessment we can find the right type of lessons for you.
